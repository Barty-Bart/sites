Casa Sicilia Cafe

Opened in late November 2024 by Sicilian owner and trained pastry chef Corrado Cannata, Casa Sicilia Caffe brings a slice of Sicily to the former Monticello Cakes & Continental Gelati shop (a High Street institution since the 1960s) — think brioche con granita breakfasts, four kinds of arancini, and cannoli filled with silky ricotta, all baked in-house daily. Featured in The Age ahead of opening, it has quickly become a Thornbury local favourite with a 4.8-star Google rating.
