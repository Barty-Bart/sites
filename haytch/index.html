Haytch

Haytch is a much-loved licensed cafe on Thornbury's High Street, run by couple Harry and Helen (hence the name) and famous for its house-made vanilla slice — the owner reportedly arrives at 3am to bake the croissants and cakes. The 58-seat space pairs earthy tones, hanging greenery and wall art with specialty coffee and a creative all-day brunch menu.
